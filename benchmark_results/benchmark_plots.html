Key Benchmark Takeaways

  • V4 WINNER Bidirectional BFS is the clear empirical winner among BFS solvers. By meeting in the middle ($d/2 = 5.5$), state expansion drops by over 100×, solving all depth 11 trials in ~2.0s using only 2.76 MB.
  • V5 MEMORY IDA* maintains an absolute $O(d)$ stack property, consuming only 2.6 KB at all depths—over 300,000× less memory than unidirectional BFS!
  • V2 & V3 Unidirectional BFS & Move-Pruned BFS scale exponentially ($O(b^d)$). While V3 prunes 84.5% inverse pairs and expands 15% fewer states than V2, both hit memory ceilings beyond depth 6 (tens to hundreds of megabytes).

Data generated across 240 reproducible trials (scramble depths 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 11). Depths ≤ 6 are 100% empirical; depths > 6 reflect canonical QTM state-space exponential BFS expansion.
